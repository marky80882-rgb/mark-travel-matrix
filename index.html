How to Choose the Right Carry-On Luggage

Choosing a carry-on can make your trip easier. Before buying one, check your airline's current size and weight limits because rules can vary by airline and ticket.

1. Check the size

Measure the suitcase, including wheels and handles. Compare its dimensions with your airline's carry-on rules before traveling.

2. Choose a suitcase style

Hard-shell luggage can help protect items from bumps, while soft-sided luggage may offer flexible pockets and a little extra give. Think about what you plan to pack.

3. Look at wheels and handles

Check whether the wheels roll smoothly and the handle feels comfortable. Spinner wheels can make it easier to move a bag in different directions.

4. Consider organization

Compartments and pockets can help separate clothing, electronics, and travel documents. Choose a layout that fits how you like to pack.

5. Compare before you buy

Review the dimensions, weight, materials, warranty, and return policy. Don't choose by appearance alone.

Travel tip: Leave a little room for items you may need during the trip, and keep important documents easy to reach.
